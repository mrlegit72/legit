## Length & shape

- Target: 500–800 words per long-form post.
- Open with a one-sentence hook that lands a concrete claim, not a question.
- Short paragraphs. Two or three sentences max.
- One idea per paragraph.

## Forbidden patterns

- No "in today's world" / "in this article we will" / "let's dive in" openers.
- No bullet lists longer than 5 items.
- No emojis unless you explicitly add them after.
- No corporate hedging ("it could be argued that", "one might consider").
- No three-em-dash sentences — pick one clean break.
- No "delve", "leverage", "robust", "harness", "unlock", "embark".

## Required moves

- At least one concrete example or specific number per piece.
- A clear point of view stated as a claim, not as a question.
- The piece should be linkable: a reader could quote one sentence as a thesis.

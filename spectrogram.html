Detected Peaks
Start the microphone to detect frequency peaks.
🎵 How to read it: The X-axis is time (scrolling left). The Y-axis is frequency — low notes at the bottom, high notes at the top. Brightness shows how loud each frequency is at that moment.
🎸 Try it with: Sing a vowel and watch the harmonics stack up. Strum a guitar string and see it ring out. Whistle to see a thin bright line. Clap to see wideband noise.
🔬 Harmonics: When you sing a note, you'll see bright lines at the fundamental frequency AND at 2×, 3×, 4× that frequency. These overtones are what make your voice sound like you.

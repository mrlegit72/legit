How well do you actually hear?

Most people have never measured their own hearing. This test sweeps your ears across the full audible range and tells you the highest frequency you can still hear, where your sensitivity drops, and how your hearing compares to a typical curve for your age.

Before you begin

For accurate results, four things matter:

  • Wear headphones or earbuds. Built-in laptop speakers can't reproduce frequencies above ~16 kHz and miss low bass entirely.
  • Find a quiet room. Background noise will mask quieter tones and skew your results.
  • Set your system volume to a comfortable level — not loud. We'll do a calibration step next.
  • Don't crank the volume mid-test. Some frequencies you can't hear; that's the point. Turning up to "find" them just damages your ears.

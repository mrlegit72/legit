What you can actually do with this

Test speakers & rooms

Sweep your monitors and walk around — where the sweep gets loudest is a room mode. Where it disappears is a null. This is the same technique that costs $200 in measurement software.

Find your hearing range

Take the 90-second hearing test and get your personal audiogram, plus a comparison to typical hearing for your age.

Tune an instrument

The Mic / Tuner mode listens to your mic and shows the dominant frequency, the nearest note, and how flat or sharp you are. Works on any sustained pitched sound.

Mask tinnitus

Notched noise around the ringing pitch can reduce its perceived loudness. Use the Noise tab and pick a flavour that's just above your tinnitus tone.

Compare A vs. B

The ABX mode hides which sound is playing and lets you guess. After 10 trials it tells you whether you actually hear a difference, or you were just guessing.

Send it through a real signal chain

Calibration tone, polarity check, L/R/centre tests — the basic stereo verification kit you used to need a CD for.

About

A free, open implementation inspired by the classic online tone generator at szynalski.com. Everything runs on your device — nothing is sent to a server. Suitable for testing speakers, headphones, ear training, tinnitus matching, and audio demos.

Tip: at very high frequencies (above 16 kHz) and very low ones (below 40 Hz) what you hear depends heavily on your hardware. If you hear nothing, your speakers may simply not reach there.
